## *The Creatures Lurk* Is Coming Soon

The wait is nearly over. *The Creatures Lurk*, the first book in *The Veilhost* series, will launch as an eBook for Kindle on **31st July**.

For readers who prefer the feel of a physical book in their hands, the paperback edition will follow shortly afterwards and will be available from **2nd August**.

Prepare to enter a world of strange creatures, hidden powers and an approaching threat that could change everything.
